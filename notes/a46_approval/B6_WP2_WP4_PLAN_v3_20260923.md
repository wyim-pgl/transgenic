## 2. WP2 — rule 5 repair (Writer A)

**Files.** `src/transgenic/evidence/b6_corpus_repair.py` (imports `build_b5`/`gsf_contract` read-only), `revision/scripts/evidence/b6_build_corpus.py` (`--base --splits --flags --build-flags --seed --measure-only|--apply --out b5_full_b6_v1.db --freeze`, exclusive, no WAL), `b6_validate_corpus.py`, `tests/test_b6_corpus_repair.py`.

**Method.** Copy A40 and update rows in place without re-tiling, so `rn`, `wid`, order and coordinates stay A40's.
1. **Enumerate edge crossers explicitly.** `gc.genes_in_window` returns `(inside, partial_count)` (`gsf_contract.py:537-542`), not the partial genes. Use the same predicate as `gsf_contract.py:541` and `leakage.py:389-390`: `edge = [g for g in genes if g.end0 > ws and g.start0 < we and not (g.start0 >= ws and g.end0 <= we)]`.
2. **Population (D1, reading recorded).** For each kept train/valid forward tile, an edge crosser is a seed if the build's `leak` predicate (`build_b5.py:567`: orthogroup split ranks above the tile split) or its `hard` predicate (`:569`: weight 0, i.e. A22 or A30) holds. Decoy is not applied, because decoys are artefact reduction, not leakage (A33.3). A strict-held-out-only population would waive the orthogroup and hard-mask obligations, so it is not an option.
3. **Closure, no partial mask (A33.3).** Mask the whole overlap component (over `inside ∪ edge`) of every edge seed. A labelled inside gene in such a component is **unlabelled**. The builder regenerates `gff` with `gc.window_to_gsf_v3` over the remaining labelled genes, recomputes tokens, `n_transcripts` and `qc`, deletes the gene's `window_genes` rows, and counts a `closure_label_drop`.
4. **One flank per component (A33.3; rule 4(a) "the exact seeded value per component").** Each newly masked overlap component gets one draw `randint(50, 150)` from `random.Random(f"{seed}:{species_id}:{chrom}:{ws}-{we}:b6edge:{first_gene_id}")`. `first_gene_id` is the component's first member in (start0, gene_id) order. The mask covers [min start0 − flank, max end0 + flank) of the component, clipped to the tile. The flank is recorded on **every member's** `b6_dna_repair` row. The builder exports `b6_corpus_repair.component_flank(seed, species_id, chrom, ws, we, component)` so the exclusion side can reproduce the value exactly; a test compares the two.
   - The A40 per-gene masks (`build_b5.py:597-599`, `gsf_contract.leak_mask` `:654-663`, verified by `leakage.py:353-367`) are left unchanged. A component that already holds A40-masked genes keeps those spans, and the repair mask is added to them.
   - The key leaves the build's `:flank` stream untouched; `leakage.py:12`'s 150 is an exclusion bound only.
5. **RC twin** = `revcomp` of the repaired forward tile, with GSF `gc.reverse_complement_v3` of the regenerated GSF.
6. **Measure first.** Two events change the row set: a train/valid tile over masked fraction 0.60 (`build_b5.py:602`), and closure removing the last labelled gene of a tile with an RC twin. `--measure-only` counts them. `--apply` refuses if either is non-zero, because the train rows would leave 198,828, the basis of **N_pilot = 4,142** (P:999); **a further amendment is then required.** Label drops alone keep the rows and are applied.

**Outputs.** `b6_dna_repair(rn, wid, species_id, chromosome, start0, end0, gene_id, component_first_gene_id, role, flank, is_rc)` and `b6_label_drop(rn, gene_id, cause)` inside the B6 DB. The freeze JSON records `base_sha256` (A40), `out_sha256` (one sha for B6 and its comparator), code sha, seed, input shas, flank key, rule id `rule5-leak-hard-component-v1`, counts by species × split × cause, and row counts.

**Acceptance.** Frozen byte identity is **B6 versus its comparator** (one file, same `rn` order). The A40 comparison is an exact declared-change audit: sequence changes are ACGT→N inside repair spans, label changes are `b6_label_drop` rows, and test tiles never change. 

**Fixture.** The world's gL is train and non-hard (`tests/test_b6_c0_world.py:16,45`), so D1 does not select it; it is the negative control. `test_b6_corpus_repair.py` builds its own variant without editing WP1's fixture. It adds `gHE`, a strict held-out test-orthogroup gene crossing a 30,720 edge, to be masked with one component flank. It adds an overlapping labelled train gene, to be dropped by closure. It adds a tile pushed over 0.60, which must make `--apply` refuse.

Also required: forward/RC consistency, byte-identical rebuilds, and 198,828 train rows on the real pronghorn run.

- **Exon.** `aligned_block` labels `exon` with `evidence_weight(family="exon")` (PacBio 1.0 / ONT 0.8 / EST 0.6) and per-base units. Retained-intron bases get 0.25.
- **Protein CDS (A19.3, P:299).** Accepted, non-ambiguous placements label **`protein_coding_gene` and `exon` over the aligned CDS blocks**, with source weight 1.0 and n = distinct organisms covering the base. CDS phase from the alignment goes to `evlabel_phase` (phase is not a class). Protein never labels UTR.
- **UTR (A50/A50.1).** Candidate placements are same-strand, every CDS block inside a read block, and CDS introns equal to the read's introns over the CDS span.
  - With one distinct (start, stop), read bases 5′ of the start codon are `5UTR` and bases 3′ of the stop are `3UTR`. These cells also carry `protein_coding_gene` at the read's weight (`_TYPE_CLASSES`). Stop-codon bases are never UTR.
  - An incomplete side is neutral (`*_blocked_incomplete_orf`). If distinct starts or stops conflict, the whole read is neutral (`*_blocked_conflicting_cds`). EST contributes interior bases only.
  - The 14 classes lack TSS/TES, so v1 emits no boundary cell (`boundary_cells_not_emitted_v1`). **This deferral is not final.** The A50.2 consumer amendment must re-decide boundary cells and the WP2c gate before the segmentation experiment trains. Until then, `evlabel_provenance` records the policy and the consumer must refuse to treat the absence of boundary cells as a decision.
- **Merging.** Max across sources. Weight = `min(4, 1 + sw·g·log1p(n))`, with genotype per D10.

**Output.** `evlabel_b6_v1.db` with `evlabel_cell(rn, class_index, start0, end0, weight, source, family, rule)`, `evlabel_phase`, `evlabel_qc` and `evlabel_provenance` (`EVLABEL_SPEC_SHA`). ### 7.1 Track spec (K = 31, P:992)
- **Channels.** For s ∈ (EST, ONT, PacBio), channels 8s…8s+7 are `cov+, cov−, cov?, donor+, acceptor+, donor−, acceptor−, mask`. Channel 24 is protein CDS log1p(organisms), unstranded. Channels 25–30 are phase (0,+),(1,+),(2,+),(0,−),(1,−),(2,−).
- **Values.** float16 log1p(molecules): coverage is clipped at log1p(64); donor/acceptor sit on the boundary base.
- **RC** (`new[c] = old[perm[c]]`, then reverse L). Each source block uses the frozen `[1,0,2,5,6,3,4,7]` (+8s). Channel 24 is identity. The phase block is `[28,29,30,25,26,27]`: strand swapped, label kept (P:992).
- **Mask.** 1 iff the evidence owner's species has an admitted library of that source in the population, including at fraction 0 and at N. It is 0 when the source or its last library is withheld, or the tile is dropped.
- **Zeroing.** All six phase channels are zeroed over the population's zero list (A48).
- **C4.** Count channels become 1.0 where > 0; masks are unchanged.

### 7.2 Chain spec and `chain-rc-v1`
- **Elements.** 512; element 0 is the dummy (mask False, index 0, features 0) in slot 0, and chains use slots 1…63 (rule 6a).
- **Slot hash, exactly rule 6a.** `h(s) = int.from_bytes(hashlib.blake2b(f"{aug_seed}\t{row}\t{epoch}\t{s}".encode("utf-8"), digest_size=8).digest(), "big")` for s ∈ 1…63; π = slots sorted by `(h(s), s)`; the chain at fill rank r gets π[r−1]. `row` is the decimal `rn`, or the §7.4 window key at evaluation, where `epoch` = 0.
- **`chain_feat`** = (`order_fwd`, `order_rev` ≤ 31 in transcription direction, `slot` 0…63, `source` 0 EST / 1 ONT / 2 PacBio, `strand` 0 + / 1 −, `units_bin = min(units, 64)`).
- **Fill.** Long reads first, then EST, sorted by units ↓, intron count ↓, tie key ↑, then **PacBio < ONT**. The last key breaks ties between identical ONT and PacBio chains, which D8 keeps separate. Tie key = `int.from_bytes(hashlib.blake2b(f"{species}\t{strand}\t{introns}".encode("utf-8"), digest_size=8).digest(), "big")`, where `introns` = `",".join(f"{d}-{a}")` over genomic (donor0, acceptor0) in transcription order. A chain that does not fit is dropped whole, and truncations are counted per source.
- **`chain-rc-v1`**, written and hashed: p → L−1−p; roles kept; strand swapped; order, slot and source unchanged. Twice = identity.
- **`CHAIN_SPEC_SHA`** covers every rule 9 item (P:997), including the §9 projections, both hash encodings and the source-order tie-break.

### 7.3 Draws (uniforms keyed to the recipient, resolved against the evidence owner)
- **Rank per draw.** Shards store `molecule_id`, never a rank. At load, the rule 8 rank is computed per requested `aug_seed`: 20260914 + seed for training (123 gives 20261037), and 20260914 + d for dose-response draw d ∈ 0…4.
- **Uniforms.** `u(p) = int.from_bytes(hashlib.blake2b(f"{aug_seed}\t{p}\t{row}\t{epoch}".encode("utf-8"), digest_size=8).digest(), "big") / 2**64`, for purposes p ∈ {`dropout, fraction, withhold_source, withhold_source_pick, withhold_library, withhold_library_pick`}.
  A `DrawSpec` holds only the recipient's six **uniforms**. Non-numeric purposes cannot collide with the slot key, and no `torch.Generator` is used (replacing `datasets.py:336-345`).
- **Resolution** happens in `resolve(draw, owner)`, where `owner` supplies the evidence owner's admitted sources and libraries. The owner is the recipient in every arm except C3, where it is the donor.
  `dropped = u_dropout < 0.5`; `fraction = {0.01, 0.1, 0.5, 1}[⌊4·u_fraction⌋]`. Source withholding fires if `u_withhold_source < 0.1` and picks `sorted(owner.sources)[⌊u·n⌋]` among EST/ONT/PacBio present (none present: recorded no-op). Library withholding works the same way over `sorted(owner.libraries)`, and withholding a source's last library zeroes its mask. EST withholding also removes phase; protein follows D7.
- **Arm comparisons** use the uniform log (hash of the uniforms per row/epoch), which is identical across all arms. Resolved identities are logged for QC only.
- **Sharing and epochs.** One resolved draw feeds both `rasterise` and `build_chains`. `B6EpochSampler` yields (rn, epoch), so persistent workers hold no state.

### 7.4 Contexts (two constructors, one type)
Both return `Context(row, species, chrom, ws, we, L, is_rc, kind ∈ {corpus, eval}, split, population, tile_exclusions, excluded_junctions, dna, dna_n_mask, zero_intervals, roles_scope, panel_locus)`.

**`corpus_context(corpus, rn, mode)`** is used for train and valid tiles. `row` is the decimal rn, and split and coordinates come from the corpus row.
- `train_track` covers train tiles without train-tiles-excluded molecules. `valid_track` covers valid tiles and admits them. Test tiles are refused.
- Chains split at 4(c) junctions, rule 4(a) tile intervals (including `edge_partial`), non-accepted junctions and tile edges.

**`eval_context(window, *, reference, panel, roles, generator)`** serves evaluation windows and needs **no corpus membership**. Z. mays has no B5/B6 corpus rows (`data/manifests/b5_excluded_species_v1.tsv:2`).
- **Verified identity.** The reference FASTA sha equals the evidence DB's `inputs.reference` sha. The panel sha is frozen (A. thaliana `b6_panels_v1.tsv` = `79367154…`, P rule 12; the Z. mays §9 locus list sha is recorded before first use), and `locus_id` is in the panel. (chrom, ws, we) must reproduce from a named generator: `centred30720-v1` = `mvp_windows.evaluation_window` (`mvp_windows.py:139`, fail-closed at edges), or `a27-tiles-v1` = `gsf_contract.tile_windows` (`:609`) at a recorded tier/offset. The window manifest is sha-bound.
- **Window key:** `row = f"eval:{species}:{chrom}:{ws}-{we}:{'rc' if is_rc else 'fw'}:{panel_id}"`. It keys every uniform and the slot hash, and contains non-digits, so it never collides with an rn.
- **Roles.** `eval_input` admits `b6_input` datasets only, by dataset/run. A. thaliana contributes `excluded_heldout` molecules whose primary placement overlaps the window's panel locus. Z. mays contributes M-EST (tracks and chains) and M-HQ18 (tracks only). A `b6_score` molecule is a hard error, and Z. mays in a corpus context is refused.
- **No rule 4 splitting and no masks** at evaluation (rule 6a; A33.3 "at inference nothing is masked"). Chains split at window edges only. DNA comes from the verified reference.
- **Z. mays evidence.** `b6_build_c0.py` needs `--corpus` for its leakage model (`:1707`) and refuses training roles for Z. mays (`:150`, `:240`). `b6_build_eval_c0.py` reuses its ingestion without a leakage model, for validation-only roles of never-training species. It writes c0-v1 under policy `eval_only_no_leakage_model` with the same PROVENANCE/sha/no-WAL contract, and the training cache refuses that policy.

**Zero lists (D4).** The producer at T covers only train_track (it drops train-tiles-excluded rows, `b6_routea_agreement.py:20-40`). `--population` writes one list per population (train_track, valid_track, eval_input) plus a QC JSON naming the population and its upstream shas. No b6_score molecule enters any list.

**C3 donor (D6).** Forward rows are sorted by `int.from_bytes(hashlib.blake2b(f"{aug_seed}\tc3\t{tier}\t{split}\t{wid}".encode("utf-8"), digest_size=8).digest(), "big")` and each is paired with the next, cyclically. The pairing is fixed, within (tier, split), with no fixed point, and twins share the donor. A (tier, split) group with fewer than 2 forward rows is a **build error**, reported with counts. The donor's evidence is resolved with the recipient's uniforms against the **donor's** admitted sources and libraries (§7.3), then RC-transformed iff the recipient is RC.

### 7.5 Observation cache
