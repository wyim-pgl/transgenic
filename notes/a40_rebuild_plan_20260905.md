Adding loci can change which tiles reach that check and the later RNG stream. This plan does not claim protection beyond the existing complete-locus eligibility rule.

### A40. Amendment v1.30 (2026-09-05) — no-CDS masking eligibility, canonical emitted-block order, and empty decoder targets

**Reason and analysis state.** This is a defect amendment to the implementation of A26/A27/A29/A33. Evidence alignments have already been produced and are retained unchanged. The existing 272,224-row merged corpus remains a superseded read-only audit object; the replacement corpus has not yet been built at the time of this draft. On 2026-09-05 the author chose #56 B (“lncRNA는 마스킹 해”) and accepted replacement of the merged frozen database, with the recommendations for #55 and #58 to be followed. No text in §§3–9 is changed. At adoption, record the actual state of B5 runs and result tables and the adoption date; do not infer their absence from this draft.

**A40.1 — separate masking eligibility from coding eligibility.** No-CDS loci in the declared annotation remain eligible for the existing A29/A33 masking and overlap-component rules while being excluded from coding labels. Their assigned split and hard flags determine masking seeds under the existing rules; component closure includes these loci and their overlapping coding neighbours. For the species realised leak+hard rate, the denominator contains all eligible loci, including eligible no-CDS loci, and the numerator contains the union of leak/hard-eligible loci in that population. The probability remains `min(0.05, m/3)`; train-only decoy candidates remain otherwise-labelable coding genes. Reference annotations, split assignments, seeded flank range, masking threshold and evaluation GFFs are unchanged. Existing complete-locus tile eligibility, other cap exclusions and pre-mask empty sampling are retained; no-CDS status alone is not a new unconditional masking seed. No-CDS rejection diagnostics now denote coding-label exclusion, not removal from the masking universe.

**A40.2 — canonical emitted-feature ordering.** The tile emitter orders emitted gene blocks by the same feature-span and canonical-text key required by the validator, rather than by outer annotation coordinates. This corrects both equal-span tie failures and decreasing emitted-span failures. The superseded corpus recorded 97 such rejected windows, comprising 13 ties and 84 decreasing spans. Replacement accounting shall identify how many survive all remaining filters and their RC contribution; 97 is not a promised row-count increment. Other caps and filters remain in force.

**A40.3 — empty decoder sequence.** The v3 empty decoder target is `<s> <empty> </s>`, with three tokens, consistent with the existing stored counter and the start/end convention for non-empty labels. Apply this tokenizer before every B5 seed using the replacement corpus. Record its source version; changing an already-started training chain requires a new labelled run under A28.

**A40.4 — one replacement and freeze.** Combine A40.1 and A40.2 in one nine-species build under tile recipe `gsf-contract-a40-v1`, with A40.3 in the associated training source snapshot. Write all outputs to new paths. Preserve the old DB, its freeze and alignment results. Before replacing the §1 object reference, validate the new merged DB and record input/source hashes, the uncommitted source patch if applicable, invocation/environment, new artifact hashes, row and membership counts, mask/rate accounting, empty/token counts and the fate of all historical ordering rejections. Retain all existing scoring universes and evaluation definitions. The new counts supersede old corpus counts only after verification; no new values are asserted by this draft.
