# B5 corpus accounting and Swiss-Prot masking — Methods insert

Reporting against `data/freeze/b5_full_v1.freeze.json`, measured 2026-09-05 with DuckDB `read_only=True`. This insert documents the existing corpus; it does not amend the frozen protocol, accept the pending #56/#58 exceptions, or redefine a scoring population.

## Labelled population

Orthogroup gene assignments and genomic block assignments define different populations. Across the nine species, 15,005 of 91,787 test-assigned genes (16.348%), 7,316 of 29,153 validation-assigned genes (25.095%), and 196,592 of 213,702 training-assigned genes (91.994%) occur in at least one stored tile label. Among 3,430 strict held-out loci, 431 occur in stored labels. Counts deduplicate `(species_id, gene_id)` across tiers, offsets and reverse complements; “ever labelled” is membership in `window_genes`. Missing labels can also reflect masking, edge exclusion, caps, or other rejected tiles, so these fractions are not measurements of block allocation alone.

The frozen corpus has 198,171 train, 25,910 validation and 48,143 test rows. Test tiles contain 54,188 unique labelled genes: 34,606 train-assigned, 4,577 validation-assigned and 15,005 test-assigned genes. Thus the 15,005 count is the nominal-test-gene intersection with stored labels, not the denominator of an unrestricted test-tile metric. Per-species coverage is in [Table S: labelled coverage](supplemental_tables/TableS_b5_labelled_coverage.csv). Full-GFF reference comparisons retain their reference-based denominators under A22/A30; evidence-support outcomes retain their frozen callability denominators. Neither is replaced by this corpus coverage count.

Checkpoint selection uses the trainer's mean of validation-batch losses (`train/train_HyenaTransgenic_RTX4090.py`), not a mean over unique validation genes. Its model loss includes weighted token cross-entropy over non-ignored targets. The empty-target convention remains the pending #58 decision; no numeric tokenizer-dependent denominator is asserted here. A paper outcome must name its scoring unit and actual eligible population; selecting a new gene-only test universe requires an author decision.

[Table S: test-gene distributions](supplemental_tables/TableS_b5_test_distributions.csv) compares labelled and unlabelled test genes within each species. Length is `gene_key_map.end0 - start0`. Transcript count uses the original GSF GFF parser before QC, caps and masking, with all nine reference SHA256 values checked against the build manifest and zero missing test gene keys. These are descriptive distributions, not a claim of representativeness or an isoform-scoring denominator. For example, labelled/unlabelled median lengths are 2,893/2,766.5 nt in P. patens and 2,663/2,475 nt in V. vinifera; their mean transcript counts are 2.935/2.693 and 1.179/1.061. The unresolved no-CDS masking issue #56 remains a limitation of the underlying corpus, including the unlabelled population.

## Swiss-Prot mapping and masking

Swiss-Prot reviewed Viridiplantae was used as a separate sensitivity resource and for reference-model caution flags under A30. The [per-species mapping table](supplemental_tables/TableS_swissprot_mapping.csv) reports entries, uniquely mapped entries, exact-sequence mappings (a subset of mapped entries), unmapped and ambiguous entries, and hard-flag counts with their units distinguished. The nine flag-file MD5 values match the frozen input manifest. Swiss-Prot masking is effectively Arabidopsis-only in this revision: 567 A. thaliana genes and one G. max gene have hard flags, with zero in the other seven species.

Rice uses MSU reference identifiers while Swiss-Prot cross-references use RAP-DB identifiers. No RAP–MSU map was available, and the author decided on 2026-09-02 not to pursue that conversion table. Rice mapped 3,784 of 5,161 entries through exact sequence identity, leaving 1,377 unmapped, and received zero hard flags. Exact identity can recover matching sequences but cannot establish a discrepant reference model without a reliable locus mapping; zero flags does not establish absence of annotation errors.

The [masked-gene detail table](supplemental_tables/TableS_swissprot_hard_masked_genes.tsv) is a byte-for-byte copy of `evidence/swissprot_hard_masked_genes_20260902.tsv` from the outer workspace: 571 curated-entry rows covering 567 Arabidopsis genes, retaining caution types, accessions and differences from the reference. Per the author decision of 2026-09-03, all remain hard-masked; no per-type relaxation was applied. This detailed audit is Arabidopsis-only and does not enumerate the soybean gene; the per-species table explicitly records that scope. Caution events (1,101 in the Arabidopsis summary), deduplicated flag rows (682), curated-entry audit rows (571), and unique genes (567) are different counts. Reference annotations and the full-GFF evaluation reference remain unchanged.
